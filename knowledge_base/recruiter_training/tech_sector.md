# Tech Sector Recruiting Guide

## Industry Context

The technology industry is:
- **Fast-paced**: Rapid innovation and change
- **Growth-oriented**: Scale and impact are priorities
- **Flexible**: Remote work, flexible hours common
- **Ownership culture**: Employees expected to own projects end-to-end
- **Learning-focused**: Continuous learning and skill development valued

## Key Competencies for Technical Roles

### Backend Engineers
1. **Technical Skills**:
   - Languages: Python, Go, JavaScript/Node.js, Java, Rust
   - Frameworks: FastAPI, Django, Express, Spring Boot
   - Databases: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, Elasticsearch
   - Cloud: AWS, GCP, Azure
   - DevOps: Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD

2. **Domain Knowledge**:
   - API design (REST, GraphQL, gRPC)
   - Microservices architecture
   - Scalability and performance
   - Database optimization
   - Security best practices

3. **Soft Skills**:
   - Problem-solving and debugging
   - Communication (across engineering, product, design)
   - Ownership mentality
   - Adaptability to changing requirements
   - Collaboration in agile environments

## Interview Strategy

### Opening Questions
1. "Tell me about your most impactful project. What problem did it solve?"
2. "What technologies excite you right now?"
3. "Walk me through how you approach a new technical challenge."

### Technical Deep Dive
1. **Architecture**: "How would you design a system to handle 1M requests/second?"
2. **Scalability**: "Describe a time you optimized a slow system. What was your approach?"
3. **Problem-Solving**: "What's the most complex bug you've debugged?"
4. **Trade-offs**: "When do you choose NoSQL vs SQL? What are the trade-offs?"

### Ownership & Impact
1. "Tell me about a project you owned end-to-end."
2. "How do you measure success in your work?"
3. "Describe a time you had to make a technical decision with limited information."

### Collaboration
1. "How do you handle disagreements about technical direction?"
2. "Tell me about working with product managers or designers."
3. "How do you do code review?"

## Red Flags

❌ **Avoid candidates who**:
- Only work in silos (tech requires collaboration)
- Can't articulate trade-offs in technical decisions
- Show no passion for technology or learning
- Blame others for project failures
- Unwilling to adapt to new technologies

## Positive Signals

✅ **Look for candidates who**:
- Discuss trade-offs and reasoning behind decisions
- Show passion for learning new technologies
- Take ownership of outcomes (good and bad)
- Mention collaboration across teams
- Talk about impact and users
- Have side projects or open-source contributions

## Motivations in Tech Sector

### Common Motivations
- **Impact**: Building products used by millions
- **Learning**: Exposure to cutting-edge technologies
- **Equity**: Potential for significant financial upside at startups
- **Culture**: Flexible, collaborative environment
- **Problem-solving**: Interesting technical challenges

### Questions to Probe Motivation
1. "What drew you to [our product/problem space]?"
2. "What's your ideal company stage? (Startup, growth, public)"
3. "How important is work-life balance vs high-growth opportunities?"
4. "Do you prefer deep technical work or broader impact?"

## Compensation Expectations

### Typical Ranges (USD, US Market)
- **Junior Engineer** (0-2 years): $90K - $130K + equity
- **Mid-level Engineer** (3-5 years): $120K - $170K + equity
- **Senior Engineer** (5-8 years): $150K - $220K + equity
- **Staff/Principal** (8+ years): $180K - $300K+ + equity

### Total Compensation at Startups
- **Base Salary**: 70-80% of total comp
- **Equity**: 0.1-1.0%+ depending on stage and role
- **Bonus**: Less common at startups (more common at big tech)

### Big Tech Compensation
- **FAANG**: $200K-$500K+ total comp (includes RSUs)
- **Heavy equity component**: 40-50% as RSUs
- **Performance bonuses**: 10-20% of base

## Cultural Fit Questions

1. "How do you stay current with new technologies?"
2. "Tell me about a time you shipped something quickly. How did you balance speed vs quality?"
3. "How comfortable are you with ambiguity?"
4. "What's your approach to on-call and production incidents?"

## Company Stage Fit

### Startups (Seed - Series A)
- **Look for**: Risk-takers, generalists, comfortable with chaos
- **Red flags**: Need for structure, risk-averse, narrow expertise
- **Sell**: Equity upside, impact, ownership, fast growth

### Growth Companies (Series B - C)
- **Look for**: Scale experience, process-oriented, team players
- **Red flags**: Prefer small teams, avoid meetings, need autonomy
- **Sell**: Balance of stability and growth, equity still valuable

### Public Companies
- **Look for**: Specialists, process-oriented, stable
- **Red flags**: Need for fast changes, hate meetings, need high autonomy
- **Sell**: Stability, established product, work-life balance, liquidity

## Technical Assessment Topics

Focus on:
1. **System Design**: Design Twitter/Uber/Instagram
2. **Algorithms**: Optimize data structures for scale
3. **Database**: When to use SQL vs NoSQL, indexing strategies
4. **API Design**: RESTful best practices, versioning
5. **Production**: Debugging, monitoring, incident response

## Next Steps After Interview

### If Strong Candidate
1. Highlight impactful projects they'd work on
2. Discuss team structure and collaboration
3. Emphasize learning opportunities
4. Mention equity and growth potential
5. Fast-track to technical interview

### If Concerns Exist
1. Probe specific technical areas with follow-up questions
2. Assess if they're coachable and eager to learn
3. Gauge cultural fit vs technical skills
4. Document concerns for hiring committee

## Sample Interview Flow

**Turn 1-3**: Background and impact of past work (7 min)
**Turn 4-7**: Technical deep dive and problem-solving (10 min)
**Turn 8-10**: Ownership and collaboration (5 min)
**Turn 11-13**: Motivations and career goals (5 min)
**Turn 14-15**: Questions for candidate, next steps (5 min)

## Success Metrics

Track:
- **Time to hire**: 15-30 days at startups, 30-45 at big tech
- **Offer acceptance rate**: 60%+ (competitive market)
- **Retention**: 75%+ staying 1+ years (lower than finance due to mobility)
- **Performance**: 70%+ meeting/exceeding in first year

---

**Remember**: Tech candidates value impact, learning, and equity. They're comfortable with ambiguity and fast-paced environments. Emphasize the problem being solved, growth opportunities, and ownership culture.
